# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Brazilian retail investors managing their own wealth. They are finance-savvy and comfortable with spreadsheets, not professional advisors. They consolidate everything in one place: bank accounts, brokerage holdings (ações, FIIs, ETFs, cripto), fixed income (CDB, LCI, Tesouro), real estate, vehicles, and liabilities. They return periodically to import and reconcile transactions, classify spending, and check where they stand. Portuguese-first, Brazilian financial context throughout.

## Product Purpose

Feed Level is a personal wealth-management SaaS. Its heart is transaction control: importing transactions (CSV, PDF, Pluggy/open finance), classifying them accurately with categories and rules, and reconciling them so the numbers are trustworthy. Clean transaction data feeds everything downstream: net-worth tracking, analytics, and goals. Success is the user trusting the totals because the underlying data is correct and easy to keep correct.

## Brand Personality

Efficient, dense, expert. The voice is quiet and competent, the way a precise financial instrument feels. It respects the user's time and intelligence: information-dense where density earns its keep, fast, keyboard-friendly, never flashy or hand-holding. Confidence through accuracy and craft, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- **Loud trading terminal.** No neon-on-black, blinking tickers, or day-trader Bloomberg energy. This is wealth tracking and reconciliation, not speculation.
- **Generic AI-SaaS template.** No purple gradients, hero-metric cards, identical icon-card grids, or the default shadcn look that makes every product interchangeable. Commit to specific, considered choices.

## Design Principles

- **Data earns trust.** Numbers are the product. Make them legible, aligned, and unambiguous before anything decorative.
- **Density with rhythm.** Pack information where the expert user wants it, but use spacing and hierarchy so dense never becomes cluttered, the legacy-bank failure mode.
- **Fast over fancy.** Prefer inline and progressive interactions over modals and detours. The user is reconciling many rows; every extra click compounds.
- **Restraint as identity.** Reference Linear and Stripe: tinted neutrals, one deliberate accent, sharp typography. Calm precision is the brand, not a color trick.
- **Correctness is visible.** Surface reconciliation state, classification status, and import provenance so the user can see the data is right, not just hope it is.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Not a focus area for the current phase. Keep reasonable defaults (visible focus states, keyboard operability, sane contrast from the existing token system) but do not invest extra effort beyond that for now.
